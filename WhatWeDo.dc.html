why we exist

There's so much music happening in Singapore right now. Bedroom producers, local concerts on every other weekend, open mics, first singles going up on Spotify every week. But for many of us youths, we're doing it alone, faced with way too much uncertainty and way too little support.

Singapore doesn't have a talent gap, but rather a lack of opportunity. Youth musicians lack a way for people to actually find each other.

So this is our attempt at fixing that. Events, spaces, whatever it takes to get young musicians and creatives in the same room and keep them coming back to each other, not just to us.

We're not trying to be another one-off gig. We want to be the reason two people who'd never have met end up making something together.

a youth movement bringing young people together through music. you don't have to be the best to belong.
